# Week 1: Foundations & Prompt Engineering

Goal: be comfortable reading/writing basic Python, and understand why the words you
send to an LLM decide the quality of the answer.

## Lessons

| # | Day | Topic | File |
|---|-----|-------|------|
| 1 | 1 | Python basics: variables, types, printing | `lessons/01_variables_and_types.py` |
| 2 | 2 | Strings & f-strings (this is how prompts are built) | `lessons/02_strings_and_prompts.py` |
| 3 | 3 | Lists & dictionaries (how data moves in and out of an API) | `lessons/03_lists_and_dicts.py` |
| 4 | 4 | Functions (wrapping a prompt so you can reuse it) | `lessons/04_functions.py` |
| 5 | 5 | Prompt engineering: role, task, context, format, examples | `lessons/05_prompt_structure.md` |
| 6 | 6 | Build a tiny prompt generator + weekend exercise | `lessons/06_mini_project.py` |

## Rules of the week
- Type the code yourself. Copy-paste teaches nothing.
- Break it on purpose, read the error, fix it. Errors are the lesson.
- Every day ends with one small change of your own.
